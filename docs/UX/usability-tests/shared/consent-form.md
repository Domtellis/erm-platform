# Participant Consent Form: AI System Validation

## Project: Intelligent ERM Platform (SB-02)

### 1. Purpose
You are being asked to participate in a usability test of an AI-augmented Risk Management platform. The goal is to evaluate how effectively the system supports human decision-making using **Gemini 2.0 Flash**.

### 2. Interaction with AI
- **Automated Suggestions**: You will interact with features that provide automated risk scoring and reasoning justifications.
- **Explainability**: You will be encouraged to review the AI's "Thinking" process to verify its accuracy against ISO 45001:2018 standards.

### 3. Data Collection & Privacy
- **Recordings**: Your screen and audio may be recorded to capture your "Think Aloud" feedback.
- **Governance Logging**: Any overrides or feedback you provide to the model will be stored in our internal **Calibration Log** for system improvement.
- **Personal Data**: No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) will be transmitted to the AI provider (Google Vertex AI) during this test.

### 4. Human Sovereignty
- You understand that the AI is a **Decision Support Tool** only.
- In this platform, a human (you) always makes the final decision. The AI cannot "lock" or "force" a decision without your confirmation.
