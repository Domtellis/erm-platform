# Product Context: Intelligent ERM Platform

This directory contains the product strategy, roadmap, and delivery artifacts for the ERM Platform.

## Vision: The Intelligent System of Governance
We are shifting from a passive "System of Record" to an active **"System of Intelligence."** By leveraging Gemini 2.0 AI, we reduce the cognitive load on risk professionals through automated triage, narrative synthesis, and evidence mapping.

## Structure
- **[01-PRDs](./01-prds)**: Product Requirement Documents defining the "What" and "Why."
- **[02-Backlog](./02-backlog)**: The executable units of work (Epics, Features, Stories).
- **[03-Metrics](./03-metrics)**: Product health and success indicators.
- **[MVP Scope](./mvp-scope.yaml)**: The definition of the "Walking Skeleton."
- **[Release Plan](./release-plan.yaml)**: The multi-horizon rollout strategy.

## Key Product Pillars
1. **Trust-by-Design**: Governance-grade audit trails for every AI and human action.
2. **Augmented Intelligence**: Human-in-the-Loop as a mandatory architectural constraint.
3. **Closing the Loop**: Continuous model calibration via human feedback loops.
