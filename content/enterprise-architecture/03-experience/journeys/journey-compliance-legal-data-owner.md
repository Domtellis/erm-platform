# Journey Map — Compliance / Legal + Data Owner

## Goals
- Ensure disclosures are approved, consistent, and defensible.
- Run data certification gates for official reporting.
- Maintain audit-ready traceability and lineage.

## Steps (end-to-end)
| # | Value stream | What the user is trying to do | Emotions | Pain points | Success criteria |
|---|-------------|--------------------------------|----------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 | VS-08 | Define reporting templates, narrative standards, and certification checks | Exacting | Inconsistent definitions | Standards adopted across packs |
| 2 | VS-08 | Perform data certification gate; block release if conflicts found | Strict | Conflicting dashboards | “One version of truth” enforced |
| 3 | VS-08 | Run external disclosure workflow with approvals and evidence | Risk-sensitive | Missing approvals | Approval chain complete |
| 4 | VS-06 → VS-08 | Retrieve evidence and provenance on demand | Demanding | Weak lineage | Complete lineage and retention |

## Moments that matter
- Certification gate catches conflicts before publication (VS-08).
- Disclosure approval chain is complete and reproducible (VS-08).

## Success criteria (measurable)
- Certification first-pass rate >= 95%.
- Disclosure cycle time predictable within policy SLA.
