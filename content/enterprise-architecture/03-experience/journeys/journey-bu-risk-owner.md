# Journey Map — Business Unit Risk Owner

## Goals
- Maintain accurate risks and assessments with minimum admin overhead.
- Implement treatments that move residual risk and meet acceptance conditions.
- Respond quickly to breaches and operational evidence.

## Steps (end-to-end)
| # | Value stream | What the user is trying to do | Emotions | Pain points | Success criteria |
|---|-------------|--------------------------------|----------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 | VS-01 → VS-02 | Convert signals / initiatives / incidents into a drafted risk | Relieved | Too much manual entry | Risk created with pre-filled links |
| 2 | VS-02 | Draft cause–event–impact; classify; assign owner/reviewer; link to objective/service/supplier | Busy | “What good looks like” unclear | Quality checks guide completion |
| 3 | VS-03 | Assess inherent → control effectiveness → residual; attach evidence; record confidence | Defensive | Evidence scattered across tools | Evidence checklist + provenance works |
| 4 | VS-04 | Evaluate vs appetite; decide response path; request funding where needed | Anxious | Slow approvals | Routing is automatic; SLAs visible |
| 5 | VS-05 | Create treatment plan + actions; define verification evidence; acceptance with expiry | Motivated | Treatments not tracked in delivery | Delivery rollup visible; verification enforced |
| 6 | VS-07 | Monitor KRIs/KCIs; respond to alerts; update posture/confidence | Urgent | False alerts; unclear escalation | Breach workflow is predictable |
| 7 | VS-08 | Provide pack updates and action commitments | Accountable | Duplicate reporting | Updates flow automatically into pack |

## Moments that matter
- One-click risk from a signal (VS-01 → VS-02).
- Guided assessment submission with evidence minimums (VS-03).
- Treatment plan auto-rollup into delivery view (VS-05).

## Success criteria (measurable)
- Identification-to-triage <= 10 business days.
- >= 85% treatment actions delivered on time.
- Appetite breach remediation time improves month-on-month.
