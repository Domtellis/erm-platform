# SB-01 Architecture Walkthrough: Appetite Breach Response

This document traces a single Appetite Breach Response flow through the architecture to verify bounded context boundaries, SoR ownership, and auditability.

### 1. Breach Detection & Case Creation
- **Action**: Monitoring Signal received (e.g., Manual submission or API stub).
- **Owner**: `Monitoring & Breaches` context.
- **Data Change**: `BreachSignal` created; `BreachCase` initialized (Status: `Detected`).
- **Audit**: `BREACH_CASE_CREATED` event emitted.

### 2. AI-Assisted Enrichment & Triage
- **Action**: AI service generates a triage and score suggestion via Gemini 2.0 Flash.
- **Owner**: `erm-ai-risk-service`.
- **Interaction**: AI Service consumes `BREACH_CASE_CREATED`, performs zero-shot reasoning.
- **Audit**: `AI_SUGGESTION_CREATED` event emitted with rationale.

### 3. Threshold Evaluation
- **Action**: System compares reported metric to appetite thresholds.
- **Interaction**: `Monitoring` queries `Appetite & Criteria` for active thresholds via Sync API/Cache.
- **Owner**: `Monitoring` context creates internal `Evaluation` record.
- **Audit**: `THRESHOLD_EVALUATED` event emitted (includes severity).

### 4. Triage & Calibration (HITL)
- **Action**: Risk Lead reviews the AI suggestion and approves/overrides it.
- **Owner**: `Monitoring` updates `BreachCase` status to `Triaged`.
- **Feedback**: Human decision is sent back to the AI Service for calibration.
- **Audit**: `CASE_TRIAGED` and `AI_CALIBRATION_FEEDBACK_CAPTURED` emitted.

### 5. Decision & Approval (The Governance Gateway)
- **Action**: Breach owner submits a response decision (AI-assisted narrative generation).
- **Owner**: `Decisioning & Approvals` context creates a `Decision` record.
- **Interaction**: `Decisioning` performs a **Separation of Duties (SoD)** check.
- **Audit**: `DECISION_SUBMITTED` then `DECISION_APPROVED` events emitted.

### 6. Evidence & Closure
- **Action**: User uploads evidence; AI verifies mapping to control health.
- **Owner**: `Evidence & Provenance` context.
- **Audit**: `EVIDENCE_ATTACHED` and `CASE_CLOSED` emitted.

---

**Verification Summary**

This walkthrough confirms that:
1. No two services write to the same entity (SoR adhered).
2. Every significant state change is captured by the Audit trail.
3. Domain boundaries (DDD) are respected.
