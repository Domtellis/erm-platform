# North Star Metric: Verified Risk Actions Closed On-Time (Weighted)

The North Star Metric (NSM) is the primary indicator of the ERM Platform's value delivery. It measures our ability to move from "Risk Awareness" to "Risk Resolution" with high integrity.

## Metric Definition
**Verified Risk Actions Closed On-Time (Weighted)**
- **Numerator**: Sum of weighted actions closed on/before due date with approved evidence.
- **Denominator**: Total weighted actions due in the period.
- **Weighting**: Actions are weighted by the criticality of the parent risk (Inherent/Residual) and the severity of the related breach.

## The Role of AI in Scaling the North Star
To achieve a "Trust at Scale" architecture, AI (Gemini 2.0 Flash) acts as the primary accelerator for this metric across three phases:

1. **Acceleration (Time-to-Action)**:
   - AI-Enriched Triage reduces the time from breach detection to action assignment by providing high-confidence suggestions to humans-in-the-loop.
   - This directly increases the likelihood of "On-Time" closure by giving owners more lead time.

2. **Quality (Evidence Integrity)**:
   - AI-Assisted Evidence Mapping validates that the uploaded evidence directly supports the closure criteria.
   - This reduces the "Rejection Rate" by approvers, preventing late-stage rework.

3. **Synthesis (Narrative Intelligence)**:
   - AI-Generated Insight Narratives ensure that the rationale for closure is professional, standard-compliant (ISO 45001), and audit-ready, increasing the "Verified" status speed.

## Guardrails
- **HITL Governance**: No AI suggestion can close an action without human verification.
- **Calibration Loop**: We monitor the "Agreement Rate" between AI suggestions and human decisions to detect model drift or bias.
