# Capability Map — AI-Enabled Enterprise Risk Management Platform

## L0 Capability
- **L0:** AI-Enabled Enterprise Risk Management Platform

---

## L1–L3 Capabilities

### L1-01. Risk Governance and Operating Model
#### L2-01.01. Governance structures and decision rights
- **L3:** Committee and cadence management (agenda packs, actions, minutes, decision log)
- **L3:** Escalation and authority matrix (thresholds, approvals, delegations)
- **L3:** Three-lines alignment (role demarcation, handoffs, oversight views)

#### L2-01.02. ERM policy, standards, and methodology
- **L3:** Policy library management (versioning, exceptions, attestations)
- **L3:** Method configuration (risk scales, scoring logic, criteria definitions)
- **L3:** ERM lifecycle definition (states, workflow, SLAs)

#### L2-01.03. Taxonomy and enterprise structures
- **L3:** Risk taxonomy management (categories, sub-types, mapping rules)
- **L3:** Organisational modelling (legal entities, BUs, regions, functions)
- **L3:** Accountability modelling (risk owners, control owners, approvers)

---

### L1-02. Risk Appetite, Risk Criteria, and Decision Guardrails
#### L2-02.01. Appetite and tolerance definition
- **L3:** Appetite statements by risk class (financial, cyber, safety, compliance, resilience)
- **L3:** Thresholds and limits (tolerances, triggers, early warning bands)
- **L3:** Conditional appetite rules (by geography, customer segment, product line)

#### L2-02.02. Risk criteria and evaluation rules
- **L3:** Impact/likelihood scales (incl. velocity, persistence, detectability)
- **L3:** Scoring model management (qualitative, semi-quant, quant)
- **L3:** Acceptability rules (acceptable / treat / avoid / transfer)

#### L2-02.03. Decision gating and approvals
- **L3:** Risk-based stage gates (investment, architecture, go-live, procurement)
- **L3:** Exception handling (waivers, time-bound approvals, compensating controls)
- **L3:** Risk acceptance management (sign-off, expiry, conditions, renewals)

---

### L1-03. Risk Sensing and Context Intelligence
#### L2-03.01. External risk intelligence
- **L3:** Horizon scanning (regulatory, geopolitical, macro, industry)
- **L3:** Threat intelligence ingestion (cyber feeds, advisories)
- **L3:** External signal triage (materiality tagging, routing)

#### L2-03.02. Internal signal capture
- **L3:** Operational telemetry ingestion (availability, incidents, performance)
- **L3:** Audit/assurance findings ingestion (issues, ratings, themes)
- **L3:** Customer/complaint signals (voice-of-customer, service quality)

#### L2-03.03. AI-enabled signal processing
- **L3:** NLP classification and deduplication (theme extraction, clustering)
- **L3:** Emerging risk detection (trend breaks, weak-signal surfacing)
- **L3:** Context packs for assessments (auto-generated briefings with sources)

---

### L1-04. Risk Identification and Portfolio Structuring
#### L2-04.01. Risk intake and capture
- **L3:** Multi-channel intake (forms, email, API, chat, ticketing)
- **L3:** Structured risk statement builder (cause–event–impact)
- **L3:** Ownership and accountability assignment (primary/secondary owners)

#### L2-04.02. Dependency mapping
- **L3:** Link risks to objectives and KPIs (strategy alignment)
- **L3:** Link to services/assets/processes (critical services, applications, sites)
- **L3:** Link to third parties (suppliers, partners, outsourcers)

#### L2-04.03. Portfolio rationalisation
- **L3:** Duplicate detection (semantic similarity, consolidation workflow)
- **L3:** Risk hierarchy (parent/child, systemic vs local)
- **L3:** Thematic clustering (by driver, control family, business domain)

---

### L1-05. Risk Assessment and Modelling
#### L2-05.01. Risk analysis
- **L3:** Inherent risk assessment (before controls)
- **L3:** Control effectiveness assessment (design vs operating)
- **L3:** Residual risk calculation (modelled and explainable)

#### L2-05.02. Quantification and scenario methods
- **L3:** Scenario definition (stress tests, tail events, plausible severe)
- **L3:** Loss modelling (ranges, distributions, Monte Carlo hooks where needed)
- **L3:** Sensitivity/assumption management (assumption logs, dominance analysis)

#### L2-05.03. Assessment quality and challenge
- **L3:** Second-line review workflow (challenge, revise, approve)
- **L3:** Evidence management (citations, attachments, data provenance)
- **L3:** Assessment QA scoring (completeness, rationale quality, consistency)

#### L2-05.04. AI-enabled assessment acceleration
- **L3:** Drafting assistance (risk narratives, drivers, impacts)
- **L3:** Control suggestion and mapping (recommended control families)
- **L3:** Explainable scoring rationales (why rating changed; what drove it)

---

### L1-06. Risk Evaluation and Portfolio Optimisation
#### L2-06.01. Appetite/criteria evaluation
- **L3:** Automated breach detection (against appetite/thresholds)
- **L3:** Prioritisation logic (materiality, velocity, concentration, confidence)
- **L3:** Escalation triggers (board/ExCo thresholds, critical service flags)

#### L2-06.02. Portfolio views and concentration
- **L3:** Heatmaps and distribution analytics (by BU, region, risk class)
- **L3:** Concentration risk analysis (single points of failure, systemic clusters)
- **L3:** Portfolio “what-if” (impact of treatments, funding scenarios)

#### L2-06.03. Risk decisioning
- **L3:** Decision log and rationale (traceability to evidence)
- **L3:** Funding alignment (link to initiatives, CAPEX/OPEX)
- **L3:** Risk acceptance / transfer decisions (formalisation and controls)

---

### L1-07. Risk Treatment and Control Design
#### L2-07.01. Treatment planning
- **L3:** Response option management (avoid/reduce/transfer/accept)
- **L3:** Treatment backlog (epics/features/stories, prioritisation)
- **L3:** Ownership, due dates, dependencies (delivery governance)

#### L2-07.02. Control library and design
- **L3:** Control taxonomy (prevent/detect/correct; manual/automated)
- **L3:** Control objectives and standards (expected evidence, frequency)
- **L3:** Control-to-risk mapping (coverage, gaps, overlaps)

#### L2-07.03. Treatment effectiveness management
- **L3:** Post-treatment reassessment (residual risk delta)
- **L3:** Compensating controls management (temporary mitigations)
- **L3:** Acceptance conditions and expiry (renewal workflow)

#### L2-07.04. AI-enabled treatment optimisation
- **L3:** Recommended treatments (based on patterns, similar risks, prior outcomes)
- **L3:** Control gap inference (where failures cluster; missing control families)
- **L3:** Action plan drafting (SMART actions, owners, milestones)

---

### L1-08. Control Operation, Assurance, and Compliance
#### L2-08.01. Control execution and evidence
- **L3:** Control execution scheduling (frequencies, calendars)
- **L3:** Evidence capture automation (APIs, logs, snapshots)
- **L3:** Evidence quality checks (completeness, freshness, tamper-evidence)

#### L2-08.02. Control testing and monitoring
- **L3:** Test plans and sampling (walkthroughs, design tests, operating tests)
- **L3:** Continuous controls monitoring (where telemetry supports it)
- **L3:** Control performance analytics (failure trends, weak controls)

#### L2-08.03. Compliance and obligation management
- **L3:** Obligations register (regs/standards/contractual commitments)
- **L3:** Control-to-obligation mapping (coverage, attestations)
- **L3:** Audit coordination (request management, PBC tracking)

#### L2-08.04. Issues, actions, and remediation
- **L3:** Findings management (severity, root cause, ownership)
- **L3:** Corrective action workflow (CAPA, validation of closure)
- **L3:** Recurrence prevention (link issues to risks/controls, lessons learned)

---

### L1-09. Risk Monitoring, KRIs, and Early Warning
#### L2-09.01. KRI/KPI instrumentation
- **L3:** Indicator catalogue (definitions, owners, data sources)
- **L3:** Threshold and banding (aligned to appetite)
- **L3:** Data pipelines (batch/stream ingestion)

#### L2-09.02. Eventing and breach management
- **L3:** Alerts and routing (who is notified; escalation path)
- **L3:** Breach triage workflows (classification, immediate actions)
- **L3:** Risk rating recalibration (auto-suggested updates; human approval)

#### L2-09.03. AI-enabled anomaly detection
- **L3:** Leading indicator detection (trend breaks, drift, weak signals)
- **L3:** Noise reduction (alert deduplication, suppression, correlation)
- **L3:** Predictive risk forecasting (probable breaches, exposure trajectories)

---

### L1-10. Reporting, Disclosure, and Decision Support
#### L2-10.01. Executive and board reporting
- **L3:** Board risk pack generation (top risks, movements, hotspots)
- **L3:** Thematic deep dives (systemic risks, concentrations, critical services)
- **L3:** Decision-focused reporting (options, trade-offs, recommendations)

#### L2-10.02. Stakeholder reporting
